# Read a company's public web profile

Read a company's public page, website, follower count and recent posts, and treat what it says as claims to check against award records.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/read-a-companys-public-web-profile

## What you get

Read a company's own public page: its website, follower count and most recent posts, with a link for each post. This is for contractors in capture, teaming and proposals who want to see how a partner or rival talks about its work before a call or a bid.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Pull the public web profile for Leidos (website leidos.com). Show its website, its
public page, its follower count and its three most recent posts with dates and links.
Flag any award or contract claim in the posts so I can check it against the record.
```

## How to read it

- Posts are the company's own public statements, not confirmed facts. Check any award claim against the G2X record.
- The $99 million figure is the company's wording. Do not quote it as an award value until you find a matching record.
- Posts mix contract news with other topics, so read them for priorities, partners and hiring, not as a contract list. Follower count shows reach, not capability, size or past performance.

## Check it

Open the website and post links in the answer and read the original wording. Then check any contract or award claim against the company's award records on G2X.

## Variations

- "Get the five most recent public posts for the company whose website is my teaming target's domain."
- "Read this company's public page, then check any award it mentions against its G2X contract history."
- "Compare the last three public posts from two rivals and list any teaming or hiring mentions."
