# Review a company's contract history

See a company's prime contract and IDV counts, the contract vehicles it holds and their dates before you team with it or bid against it.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/review-a-companys-contract-history

## What you get

Review one company's federal contracting portfolio: how many prime contracts and IDVs it holds, and which contract vehicles show up. This is for contractors in capture, pricing and teaming who want to know a partner's or rival's track record before a decision.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Show me the federal contract history for the company with UEI PFSLMY1VAK71. How many
prime contracts and IDVs does it hold, which contract vehicles are listed, and which
agencies issued them? Tell me anything the result could not show.
```

## How to read it

- The counts describe the company's broader history, and the list shows only a few vehicles, so the 14 IDVs are not all shown. Ask for a narrower view, such as one NAICS or vehicles ending soon, to see more.
- In this capture the individual contracts could not be listed, only the counts and vehicles. If that happens, ask your agent again, and do not infer contracts from the counts.
- Two vehicles show 2026-10-29, the day of this capture, so confirm what the date means on the record before you plan around it.
- Contract history shows what the company won as a prime. It does not show quality of performance, capacity or pricing. Check the official record before you rely on a figure.

## Check it

Look up the company on G2X, [BCT PARTNERS LLC](https://g2x.com/app/companies/PFSLMY1VAK71), and compare the counts and vehicles. Before you cite a vehicle in a proposal, confirm its status with the contracting agency.

## Variations

- "Show this company's awards in NAICS 541512 for fiscal years 2024 to 2026."
- "Which of this company's contract vehicles end within the next 12 months?"
- "Show only this company's 8(a) set-aside awards."
