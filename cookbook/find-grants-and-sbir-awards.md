# Find grants and SBIR topics

Search SBIR and STTR topics and federal grant opportunities by technology area with your AI agent, and get agencies, deadlines and record links.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/find-grants-and-sbir-awards

## What you get

SBIR and STTR topics and federal grant opportunities that match a technology area, with agencies, deadlines and links. This is for contractors, especially small businesses and research teams looking for funding outside regular procurement.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Search SBIR and STTR topics about autonomous systems. For each, show the
agency, topic number, type, response deadline and the SBIR.gov topic
link. Then search the G2X grants dataset for the same phrase and show the
G2X link for each result. Say how many matches each search reported.
```

## How to read it

- The count of 57 topics is a floor: more may match. Not every title contains the phrase, so read each topic before you rely on the title.
- The rows are topics and funding opportunities. This recipe does not show who won past awards.
- A topic can list more than one deadline, and an earlier one may come first. Read every deadline on the topic page.
- Two grant rows share a title and agency but are separate records. Open both to see how they differ.
- Confirm the exact due date and time on the topic page, and confirm eligibility with the funding agency's announcement before you plan a proposal.

## Check it

Open the SBIR.gov topic link on a row and check its topic number, agency, type and deadline. Open a G2X grant link and check the agency and description.
