# Find teaming partners with a certification

See which potential teaming partners in one NAICS hold the most contracts, with obligated dollars and any certifications on record.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/find-teaming-partners-with-a-certification

## What you get

See which firms in your NAICS hold the most contracts and which have small business or socioeconomic certifications on record. This is for contractors in capture and teaming who need a partner to meet a set-aside or add past performance.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Find potential teaming partners in NAICS 541512. Rank them by contracts won in recent
fiscal years and show contract counts and obligated dollars. Tell me which ones have
certifications on record and which ones could not be checked.
```

## How to read it

- "Certifications checked: no" means the check could not be made this time. It does not mean the company has no certification. Ask your agent again, or check that company on its own.
- Large contract counts show experience in the code. They do not show that a firm will team with you or has capacity.
- Confirm a certification is current, and that it fits the set-aside you are pursuing, before you name the firm in a plan. Check the official record before you rely on a figure.

## Check it

Open a partner's G2X page from the link in the answer, for example [BCT PARTNERS LLC](https://g2x.com/app/companies/PFSLMY1VAK71), and review its awards. Then confirm its certification on the official record.
