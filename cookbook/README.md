# GovCon MCP cookbook

Step-by-step recipes for the GovCon MCP. Each one has a prompt to paste into your AI agent, what you get back, and how to check it. They mirror the cookbook in the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook

- [Apply the rule of two with market data](./apply-the-rule-of-two-with-market-data.md): Gather candidate firms and recorded socioeconomic status into one evidence table for a rule of two review. The decision stays with the contracting officer.
- [Ask a data question in plain English](./ask-a-data-question-in-plain-english.md): Ask a procurement spending question in everyday words and get a ranked answer from your AI agent, then check the numbers yourself.
- [Benchmark labor rates for a bid](./benchmark-labor-rates-for-a-bid.md): Get the spread of published ceiling rates for one labor category, from lowest to highest with the median and quartiles, before you price a bid.
- [Read a compliance matrix for a solicitation](./build-a-compliance-matrix.md): Ask your AI agent for the compliance matrix on a federal solicitation, see each requirement's status, or learn that none exists yet.
- [Build a recompete list](./build-a-recompete-list.md): List contracts ending in the next 12 months for a NAICS code, each with the open opportunities that could be its recompete.
- [Build an IGCE with labor rate benchmarks](./build-an-igce-with-labor-rate-benchmarks.md): Pull published per-hour ceiling rate statistics for a labor category to inform the labor lines of an independent government cost estimate.
- [Check a company's socioeconomic status](./check-a-companys-socioeconomic-status.md): Look up a company's socioeconomic certifications by UEI, such as 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB and WOSB, and see what G2X can and cannot confirm.
- [Check prior awards for a similar requirement](./check-prior-awards-for-a-similar-requirement.md): Search federal contract awards by NAICS code and wording to see how comparable work was awarded, set aside and funded, for your acquisition plan.
- [Choose a contract vehicle from usage data](./choose-a-contract-vehicle-from-agency-usage.md): See how a multiple-award vehicle is used, with contract holders, task orders and dollars obligated, before you pick an ordering vehicle.
- [Find 8(a) and HUBZone firms in a state](./find-8a-and-hubzone-firms-in-a-state.md): Search the company directory for firms with 8(a) or HUBZone in their name, narrow them by state, then confirm each certification before you team.
- [Find awards by PSC code](./find-awards-by-psc-code.md): List federal contract awards that carry one product or service code, with the awardee, dates and dollars, and see how many match before you narrow.
- [Find companies by capability and NAICS](./find-companies-by-capability-and-naics.md): Search the G2X company directory by name or UEI, then read each firm's primary NAICS, state and federal award count to build a teaming shortlist.
- [Find contracts expiring next year](./find-contracts-expiring-next-year.md): List federal contracts whose period of performance ends in a date window, by agency and NAICS code, so you can plan capture before the recompete.
- [Find DIBBS parts opportunities by NSN](./find-dibbs-parts-solicitations-by-nsn.md): Ask your AI agent to find a National Stock Number from an item name, read what the item is, and open the DIBBS opportunity search for that part.
- [Find GovCon events and industry days](./find-govcon-events-and-industry-days.md): Ask your AI agent for upcoming industry days, conferences and agency events, with dates, locations and organizers, so you know where to meet buyers.
- [Find grants and SBIR topics](./find-grants-and-sbir-awards.md): Search SBIR and STTR topics and federal grant opportunities by technology area with your AI agent, and get agencies, deadlines and record links.
- [Find labor categories for a role](./find-labor-categories-for-a-role.md): Search a role in plain words, such as cloud engineer, and see which published labor category titles match, with each ceiling rate per hour.
- [Find open opportunities by NAICS code](./find-open-opportunities-by-naics.md): Ask your AI agent for open federal opportunities under one NAICS code and get titles, types, agencies, set-asides and due dates with G2X record links.
- [Find recompetes at one agency office](./find-recompetes-at-one-agency.md): Check one contracting office for contracts ending within a year in your NAICS code, with the open opportunities that could be their recompetes.
- [Find SBIR and STTR topics](./find-sbir-sttr-topics.md): Ask your AI agent for SBIR and STTR topics in a technology area, with the agency, topic number, deadline and the SBIR.gov topic page for each.
- [Find small business set-aside opportunities](./find-small-business-set-aside-opportunities.md): Ask your AI agent for open federal opportunities in your NAICS code that list a small business set-aside, with agency, type and response deadline.
- [Find sources for a national stock number](./find-sources-for-a-national-stock-number.md): Identify the item behind a national stock number and the part numbers and catalog holders on record, as a starting point for supply market research.
- [Find sources sought opportunities at one agency](./find-sources-sought-opportunities.md): Ask your AI agent for open sources sought opportunities from one agency, with NAICS codes, solicitation numbers and due dates, so you can answer early.
- [Find state and local bids](./find-state-and-local-bids.md): Ask your AI agent for open state and local bid opportunities by work type, with the issuing agency, deadline and the portal link where the bid is posted.
- [Find subcontractors for a recompete](./find-subcontractors-for-a-recompete.md): Build a shortlist of subcontractor and teaming candidates for a recompete, with contract counts, obligated dollars and SBA certifications.
- [Find teaming partners with a certification](./find-teaming-partners-with-a-certification.md): See which potential teaming partners in one NAICS hold the most contracts, with obligated dollars and any certifications on record.
- [Find the contract vehicles an agency uses](./find-the-contract-vehicles-an-agency-uses.md): Check how a contract vehicle is used, holders on record, task orders and dollars obligated, and pick the right vehicle when a name fits several.
- [Find the offices buying your service](./find-the-offices-buying-your-service.md): See which contracting offices obligated the most money under your NAICS code, so you know which buyers to research and meet first.
- [Find capable small businesses for market research](./market-research-find-capable-small-businesses.md): Build a first list of candidate firms from the G2X company directory, with primary NAICS code, state and federal award history, for market research.
- [Plan an industry day with event data](./plan-an-industry-day-with-event-data.md): Search the G2X events calendar for upcoming industry days, so you can pick a date that avoids conflicts and see how other offices run theirs.
- [Prepare solicitation documents for review](./prepare-solicitation-documents-for-review.md): Have your AI agent get one active solicitation's documents ready and tell you which can be read now, so you know what to analyze before you commit time.
- [Pull text from a solicitation attachment](./pull-text-from-a-solicitation-attachment.md): Ask your AI agent to read one solicitation attachment and quote its scope, set-aside and amendment dates exactly, so you can rely on exact wording.
- [Read a company's public web profile](./read-a-companys-public-web-profile.md): Read a company's public page, website, follower count and recent posts, and treat what it says as claims to check against award records.
- [Read a contract award record](./read-a-contract-award-record.md): Open one known federal contract award and read the awardee, agency, key dates and dollar figures, with what each amount means.
- [Read an event in detail](./read-an-event-in-detail.md): Open one GovCon event from your AI agent to see its dates, location, organizer, summary and registration link before you decide to attend.
- [Read the documents on a federal solicitation](./read-solicitation-documents.md): Ask your AI agent to list the solicitation, attachments and links filed on a federal opportunity, and see which ones it can read for you.
- [Research the incumbent on a recompete](./research-the-incumbent-on-a-recompete.md): Pull an incumbent contractor's federal award history and held contract vehicles by UEI, so you know who you are up against before you bid.
- [Review a company's contract history](./review-a-companys-contract-history.md): See a company's prime contract and IDV counts, the contract vehicles it holds and their dates before you team with it or bid against it.
- [Review a vendor's federal award history](./review-a-vendors-federal-award-history.md): See a vendor's prime award counts, indefinite-delivery contracts held and recent awards by UEI, as one input to a past performance review.
- [Scan agency procurement forecasts](./scan-agency-procurement-forecasts.md): Ask your AI agent for planned federal buys by NAICS code and fiscal year, with anticipated award dates, set-asides, estimated values and incumbents.
- [Search across G2X records](./search-across-g2x-records.md): Search G2X by keyword for contract vehicles, awards, forecasts and more, and get a short list of matches with links to the G2X records.
- [See a company's published labor rates](./see-a-companys-published-labor-rates.md): Pull one company's published ceiling rates by its UEI, with labor category, education, experience, worksite and vehicle, to size up a rival or a partner.
- [See how an agency spends in your NAICS code](./see-how-an-agency-spends-in-your-naics.md): See how one agency's contract dollars and award counts in a NAICS code split by set-aside type for a fiscal year, with one plain-language question.
- [See what similar offices bought](./see-what-similar-offices-bought.md): See which contracting offices buy services under your NAICS code, then read one office's awards to compare scope, period and dollars with your plan.
- [Size up a rival before you bid](./size-up-a-rival-before-you-bid.md): Combine a rival's contract history with its public signals to see its contract vehicles, issuing agencies and positioning before you decide to bid.
- [Summarize a solicitation](./summarize-a-solicitation.md): Have your AI agent start a requirements summary for one active solicitation, covering what offerors must do, submit or meet, with sources.
- [Track task orders on a vehicle](./track-task-orders-on-a-vehicle.md): See how many task orders sit under one IDV award, how many dollars were obligated on them, and review the orders with dates and amounts.
- [Verify a vendor's socioeconomic status](./verify-a-vendors-socioeconomic-status.md): Read the socioeconomic certifications on record for a vendor by UEI, as a quick first look before you confirm status in the official records.
- [Watch a company's public signals](./watch-company-signals.md): Check public web and news signals for a company, such as acquisitions and teaming news, each with its source link, before a call or a bid decision.
