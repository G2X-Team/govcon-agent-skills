# Find GovCon events and industry days

Ask your AI agent for upcoming industry days, conferences and agency events, with dates, locations and organizers, so you know where to meet buyers.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/find-govcon-events-and-industry-days

## What you get

Upcoming industry days, conferences and agency events, each with its dates, location and organizer, so you know where buyers will be. This is for contractors: business development, capture and teaming staff who plan which events to attend.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Find upcoming industry days on the G2X calendar. For each one, show the
title, start and end date, location and organizer, and give me its G2X
page link. Tell me how many events match in total.
```

## How to read it

- Start and end dates can differ by weeks. The last row runs from 2026-11-17 to 2027-01-13, so confirm which session you plan to attend.
- Location is either Virtual or a city. Confirm the venue with the organizer.
- A list row carries no write-up. Use [Read an event in detail](./read-an-event-in-detail.md) for the summary and registration link.
- A search that finds nothing does not mean no event is happening. Confirm the date and format with the organizer before you travel or register.

## Check it

Open an event's G2X page from its title in the table. Registration happens on the organizer's site, so confirm the date and format there before you commit.
