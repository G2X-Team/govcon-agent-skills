# See what similar offices bought

See which contracting offices buy services under your NAICS code, then read one office's awards to compare scope, period and dollars with your plan.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/see-what-similar-offices-bought

## What you get

Find which contracting offices bought the kind of work you are planning, then read a sample of what one of them awarded. This is for contracting officers, specialists and program offices who want a peer comparison for market research and acquisition planning.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
I am planning a janitorial services requirement under NAICS 561720. Which
contracting offices had the most award activity under that code in fiscal
year 2025? Show the top 5 with office code, dollars obligated and number of
awards. Then list 3 FY2025 awards from the second office on that list, with
the awardee, period of performance and obligations, and tell me where the
two views disagree or leave things out.
```

## How to read it

- The list is sorted by dollars, so it favors offices that bought large or many-site requirements. It does not tell you which office's requirement most resembles yours, so use it to choose whom to compare, then read their awards. Award counts in the list and the awards listed for one office can differ, so quote the awards when you cite specific ones.
- The data does not show scope, performance work statements, price evaluation or past performance. For those, ask the office or read the contract file. Contracting strategy, set-aside decisions and price analysis stay with your office. Check the official record before you rely on a figure.

## Check it

Open the G2X award pages from the links in the answer and compare dates and amounts with what your agent reported.
