# Find state and local bids

Ask your AI agent for open state and local bid opportunities by work type, with the issuing agency, deadline and the portal link where the bid is posted.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/find-state-and-local-bids

## What you get

Open state, county, city and school district bids for the kind of work you do, with the agency that issued each one and the portal where it is posted. This is for contractors who want to add state and local work to a federal pipeline.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).
The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Find open state and local bids for building renovation construction. For each
one, give the issuing agency, the response deadline and the portal link. Tell me
what I need to do at the portal before I can respond.
```

## How to read it

- Each bid is posted on the issuing agency's own portal. Open the portal link to see the documents and the instructions for responding.
- A portal may ask for a free or paid vendor registration before it shows the documents or takes a response. Its own instructions apply, and portals change. G2X does not register, pay or submit a bid on a portal for you.
- Deadlines are shown at 00:00 UTC, so a bid due on a given day may close earlier in your time zone. Read the exact time on the portal.
- The count of 955 is a floor: more bids may match.

## Check it

Open each portal link in the table and confirm the deadline, the issuing agency and the registration steps.
