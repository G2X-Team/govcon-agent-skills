# Find sources sought opportunities at one agency

Ask your AI agent for open sources sought opportunities from one agency, with NAICS codes, solicitation numbers and due dates, so you can answer early.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/find-sources-sought-opportunities

## What you get

The open sources sought opportunities from one agency, so you can send a capability statement while the requirement is still being shaped. This is for contractors in capture and business development who want to get on an agency's radar early.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).
The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Find open sources sought opportunities from the Department of Veterans Affairs. Show
the title, type, NAICS code, solicitation number, response deadline and
the G2X link, soonest deadline first.
```

## How to read it

- A sources sought opportunity is market research. It asks which firms can do the work and in what size category. It is not a solicitation and you cannot submit an offer to it.
- A result can carry "Sources Sought" in its title but be a different type. Check the type column in your own results.
- The response deadline is the date for your capability response, not an award date. Late responses may not be considered. No set-aside was listed on any of the five rows: agencies often decide set-asides after they read the responses, so your response can shape that decision.
- Five rows is a sample for one agency. Add a NAICS code or a date range to narrow the list.

## Check it

Open the G2X page on a title, then read the opportunity for its capability questions and its point of contact instructions.
