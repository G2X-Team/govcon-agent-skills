# Read the documents on a federal solicitation

Ask your AI agent to list the solicitation, attachments and links filed on a federal opportunity, and see which ones it can read for you.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/read-solicitation-documents

## What you get

See what is filed on an opportunity before you commit to reading it: each document's title, its category and whether its text can be read. This is for contractors in capture and proposals who need to know quickly whether the full package is available.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
List the documents on file for the Seaport CTF Design Services Follow On
solicitation (N6449826R0020). For each one, tell me its title, category,
whether its text can be read, and whether it is a file or a link.
```

## What you get back

Captured from the live GovCon MCP on 29 October 2026.
Excerpt.

| Title | Category | Text available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PIEE Solicitation Module Link for N6449826R0020 | link | yes |
| Registration Instructions for the PIEE Solicitation Module | link | no |
| Web Based Training for the PIEE Solicitation Module | link | no |

## How to read it

- Every document listed is a link posted on the opportunity. None is an attached statement of work or a pricing sheet. On this opportunity the solicitation package is reached through the PIEE Solicitation Module.
- "Text available: yes" means your agent can read the document now. A short document list is not a sign the opportunity is thin, because some agencies keep the package behind a portal that needs its own registration.
- The list can be shorter than the posting on the agency site. Do not bid from a summary: read the solicitation on the agency portal, including amendments.

## Check it

Open the G2X page for the opportunity from the link in the answer and compare its document list with the table. Then use the registration and training links the opportunity lists on the agency side to get into the solicitation module.

## Variations

- "Which documents on the Keyport Infrastructure Security Support Services opportunity can you read right now?"
- "List the amendments filed on solicitation N0040627R0003 and tell me which are new."
- "Show me only the documents on this opportunity that are files, not links."
