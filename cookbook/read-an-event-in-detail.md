# Read an event in detail

Open one GovCon event from your AI agent to see its dates, location, organizer, summary and registration link before you decide to attend.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/read-an-event-in-detail

## What you get

Read one event end to end: dates, location, organizer, a short write-up and the organizer's registration link. This is for contractors deciding whether an industry day or conference is worth the trip.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).

The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Open the NSWCPD hybrid industry day from the G2X calendar. Tell me the
date, where it is held, who organizes it, what it is about and how to
register. Give me the G2X page link and the registration link.
```

## How to read it

- The summary tells you the format: hybrid, with an in-person session at the Philadelphia Navy Yard and a virtual option. The stated focus is contracting and subcontracting opportunities, so it is most useful if you pursue Navy work.
- The answer has no agenda or speaker list, so check the organizer's page. The summary does not tell you which opportunities or contracts the agency will discuss.
- G2X does not register you. The registration link goes to the organizer's ticket page. Check the official record before you rely on a date or format.

## Check it

Open the G2X page, [FY27 Naval Surface Warfare Center Phil Div (NSWCPD) Hybrid Industry Day](https://g2x.com/app/events/fy27-naval-surface-warfare-center-phil-div-nswcpd-hybrid-industry-day), and the registration link to confirm the date, the format and any registration steps.
