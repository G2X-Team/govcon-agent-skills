# Find SBIR and STTR topics

Ask your AI agent for SBIR and STTR topics in a technology area, with the agency, topic number, deadline and the SBIR.gov topic page for each.

This recipe is also on the G2X docs: https://g2x.com/docs/cookbook/find-sbir-sttr-topics

## What you get

SBIR and STTR topics in a technology area, each with its agency, number, deadline and official topic page. This is for small business contractors and research teams deciding which R&D topics are worth a proposal.

## Before you start

Connect your AI agent with [Connect your AI agent](https://g2x.com/docs/connect).
The GovCon MCP is included on every G2X plan, from Community Edition through Enterprise.

## Prompt to paste

```text
Find SBIR and STTR topics about cybersecurity. For each topic, give the agency,
topic number, program, response deadline and the SBIR.gov topic page.
```

## How to read it

- A topic is a research area an agency has asked small businesses to propose against. It is not a contract and it does not list the full proposal instructions.
- All three topics show the same deadline, 2027-07-07. Confirm it on the SBIR.gov topic page before you plan a proposal around it.
- Three results means three found, not that no other agency has a cybersecurity topic. Add an agency name to your prompt, such as the Air Force or Navy, to see topics from a specific funding agency.

## Check it

Open the SBIR.gov topic page linked on each topic and confirm the agency, the topic number and the deadline.

## Variations

- "Find SBIR topics about autonomous systems from the Department of Defense and sort them by deadline."
- "Find STTR topics on medical devices and tell me which ones list a university research partner requirement."
- "Search SBIR and STTR attachment text for 'Phase I' budget limits on quantum sensing topics."
